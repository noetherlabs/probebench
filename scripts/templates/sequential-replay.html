Model, display and evidence

The simulator uses a static, monochromatic circular pupil and six OPD modes. All policies receive public optical calibration and noisy camera observations. Wavefront error and Strehl shown here are computed by the evaluator from latent simulation state. The phase-diversity estimator uses an exactly matched model.

Pixel display: log1p(max(measured electrons, 0)) / log1p(expected photons), with the magma colour map and fixed limits 0–1. Signed raw measurements remain in the source dataset. Commands are OPD; ideal reflective surface displacement is half that value.

The record contains all development outcomes. Related fixtures and shared noise streams do not establish population rankings, telescope performance, or agent capability. This replay is self-contained and makes no network requests.
